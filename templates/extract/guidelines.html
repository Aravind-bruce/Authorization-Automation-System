💡 How to Maximize Approval Likelihood

✅ Always include ICD-10 codes — "E11.9" is more powerful than "diabetes"
✅ Add CPT codes — procedure codes enable automated coverage matching
✅ Document step-therapy failure explicitly — name the drug, dose, duration, and exact failure reason
✅ Cite a clinical guideline — "Per 2024 ADA Standards, HbA1c >9% requires insulin" doubles approval odds
✅ Include lab values — HbA1c, eGFR, LDL, BNP relevant to the diagnosis justify treatment intensity
✅ Sign the document — unsigned submissions are auto-rejected at most payers
✅ For specialty drugs — always include TB screen, HepB/C serology, and baseline CBC/LFT
